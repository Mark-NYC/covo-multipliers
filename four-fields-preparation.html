Your discovery study

Read Mark 1–4.

Use the Four Fields Discovery Worksheet to write down what you observe. Look for specific actions, words, people, and Scripture references. Don't force answers where the text is silent.

The four fields are Entry, Gospel, Discipleship, and Church, with Leaders at the center of multiplication.

Download the Worksheet (PDF)

Four Fields Discovery Questions

  1. ENTRY: Where did they go into new places with new people, and how did they engage lostness?
  2. GOSPEL: How did they share the gospel (message content)?
  3. DISCIPLESHIP: How did they equip new believers to start obeying Jesus?
  4. CHURCH: How did they gather believers together and equip them to become a healthy church?
  5. LEADERS: How did they identify leaders from the harvest and equip them while handing over the authority, responsibility, and skills needed to multiply the entire process?

Also look for

  • What is the role of PRAYER?
  • What is the role of the HOLY SPIRIT?
  • What is the role of KINGDOM CONFLICT?
  • Did any locations see #NoPlaceLeft (the gospel got out to an entire area)?
