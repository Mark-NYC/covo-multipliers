01 / Watch

What are the Four Fields?

The Four Fields is a simple way to see the connected work of multiplication: entering new places and relationships, sharing the gospel, making obedient disciples, and gathering believers into healthy churches.

Leadership is in the center. Leaders are identified from the harvest, equipped, and entrusted to multiply the whole process.

Watch with this question in mind: Where can I see these patterns in the life and ministry of Jesus?

Four Fields overview · Plays directly on this page.
