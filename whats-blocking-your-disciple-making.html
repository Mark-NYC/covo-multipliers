Why this lab

You May Not Need More Training.

You may know the tools, pray faithfully, and care deeply about multiplication. Growth usually isn't about doing more — it's about knowing where to stay faithful with confidence and where to build competence. This lab helps you see that clearly.

Faithful, but unsure it's working

The assessment

See Where You're Strong — and Where You'll Grow.

Look at four connected areas to see where to stay faithful with confidence and where to grow in competence.

Part One Head

Vision, biblical grounding, and the ability to learn and adjust.

Part Two Heart

Love for Jesus, Spirit-led prayer, and perseverance.

Part Three Hands

Reproducing practice and developing other disciple-makers.

Part Four Harvester

Sharing locally, gathering church, and laboring with others.

A–F

This is a private snapshot to guide your growth — not a score of your worth, faithfulness, or calling.
