Are you a covo multiplier?

Most Christians have been taught to split life into compartments. Work here. Church there. Family over here. Mission somewhere else.

What we mean by covo.

The people around you are already accessible to you in a way they may never be to a pastor, missionary, or church program.

Your neighborhood, workplace, friendships, and routines are not distractions from mission. They are often the front door.

One life, not two.

Work and mission do not have to live in separate boxes.

Where you live, work, and play.

The people around you in everyday life are often the people you already have access to.

Rhythms over inspiration.

We want practices that can actually be lived and passed on.

Two places people get stuck.

Most leaders are not short on desire. They hit one of two walls.

Wall 01

You do not yet see it.

You are not sure how disciple making fits into your actual week.

Wall 02

You see it, but don’t know what to do.

You need language, models, habits, rhythms, and skills.

→ That is why the pathway starts with exposure, not just more information.

Covo Immersions

You do not need to imagine this. Come see it.

Most leaders leave training with language, tools, and good intentions, but still feel stuck when they step back into their actual week.

  • ✓ Step into real homes, workplaces, neighborhoods, businesses, meals, conversations, and debriefs.
  • ✓ See covocational disciple-making up close instead of trying to picture it from a diagram.

One lie we are trying to break

If you do not get paid for it, it does not count.

We do not buy that. Some of the most important kingdom work in your life may be the very thing you give away.

The pathway.

Four steps, from seeing your life differently to handing it to someone else.

  1. 01

    See your real life differently

  2. 02

    Learn simple habits and rhythms that fit a normal week

  3. 03

    Practice alongside others

  4. 04

    Pass it on

Why tribe matters.

This kind of life is hard to build alone.

It helps to be around other people who are actually trying to live this way, learning in public, asking honest questions, and practicing together.
