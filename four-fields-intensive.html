The real problem

Good Intentions Don’t Multiply Disciples

You care about the Great Commission. You’ve had good spiritual conversations, maybe led a Bible study, maybe been to a training or two.

But without a clear path, disciple making stays random. A good conversation here. A study there. Very little that reproduces — and almost nothing that becomes a church that plants churches.

The problem isn’t your heart. It’s that nobody handed you the whole path — from the first conversation to maturing leaders — in a form you can actually walk, and then give away.

If nothing changes

Next Year Will Probably Look a Lot Like This Year

  • Your neighbors, co-workers, and friends stay people you pray for but never get to a next step with.
  • The disciple making that does happen depends on you, and stops when you get busy.
  • Church stays something people attend instead of something ordinary disciples can start.
  • Emerging leaders around you stay spectators because no one is training them.

One weekend won’t fix all of that. It will give you a Biblical foundation, a clear process, and tools you’ve actually practiced — so you can stop drifting and start walking the path with the people already in your life.

The Kingdom clear path

One Path Through the Four Fields

Jesus used farming to describe how the Kingdom grows. The Four Fields turns that picture into a simple, reproducible path from people who don’t yet know Jesus to healthy churches led by maturing leaders. Over the weekend we’ll walk every step of it — focused on the fields where you live, work, and play.

Empty field Go Where has God already placed you?

Identify the people and places in your normal week and learn how to enter them with purpose.

Seeding field Gospel How do you sow broadly and clearly?

Move everyday conversations toward Jesus and share the gospel in a way others can repeat.

Growing field Grow How do new believers mature?

Help people obey what they learn from Scripture right away, and pass it on to others.

Harvest field Gather How do disciples become a church?

See how ordinary disciples form healthy, simple churches around Scripture, prayer, obedience, and mission.

Multiplying Guide How do leaders multiply the pattern?

Develop emerging leaders who repeat the whole path, so the work doesn’t depend on you.
